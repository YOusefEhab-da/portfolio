I study Accounting & Finance at the Faculty of Commerce (English Section), so I read numbers the way an accountant does: every figure has to reconcile, and every metric has to mean something to the business.

Through the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI) I added the technical side: SQL, Python, Power BI and advanced Excel. I take spreadsheets that arrive broken, duplicated or inconsistent, clean them, model them, and present them in dashboards that decision-makers can read in a minute.

My internships at Banque Misr and the National Bank of Egypt showed me how finance teams work day to day: tight deadlines, strict accuracy, and reports that people act on.

Clients get precision with margins, costs and revenue trends, plus clear recommendations on what to do about them.

How I work

  1. 1

    Understand the question

    We agree what decision the data needs to support, and which numbers matter.

  2. 2

    Clean the data

    Duplicates, gaps and inconsistent formats are fixed and documented.

  3. 3

    Model and analyze

    Metrics are built on a sound data model so every figure reconciles.

  4. 4

    Build the dashboard

    An interactive report in Power BI or Excel that answers the question at a glance.

  5. 5

    Deliver and explain

    A short summary of what happened, why, and what to do next.
